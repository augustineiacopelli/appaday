Where I'm headed

I'm a higher education leader moving into business and data analytics, with a particular focus on putting AI to practical work. I'm looking for roles where I can turn messy operational data into clear decisions and build the tools that make that repeatable. I'm open to analytics, AI enablement, and data driven operations roles, including remote positions.

What I've done

I have spent more than twenty years at Wichita State University, starting as an administrative assistant in 2003 and growing into the Director of Branch Campuses. Along the way I built the systems my team runs on: a suite of Google Apps Script web apps for staff scheduling, work orders, and building audits, tied together by a single sign on portal, with automated notifications and OCR card intake. I serve on the executive board of the National Association of Branch Campus Administrators, where I am First Vice Chair of the Membership Committee, and I have presented at its annual conference (2025).

I have also taught meteorology at Wichita State since 2011, including courses in severe and extreme weather. I hold a BS in Meteorology from Valparaiso University and an MBA from Wichita State, where I am now completing a Master of Science in Business Analytics, expected in 2027.

Why AppADay

AppADay began on May 8, 2026, inspired by Jonathan Mann's Song A Day. Every day, one complete, working, mobile friendly app gets designed, built, and published, with no skip days. It is my working lab for analytics and applied AI. Signal Desk, App 100, is a bring your own CSV analyst workbench with automatic profiling, driver analysis, forecasting, and Simpson's paradox checks. Semester Forecast has Claude read every syllabus in a term, then computes a week by week workload chart that names the crunch weeks before they arrive. Saga Studio grew from zero lines into a full RPG creation platform in five days. Dozens of the apps call Claude directly, each designed so the model works from computed facts rather than guesses.

What drives me

I play to win. Competition is my top CliftonStrength, followed by Significance, Consistency, Relator, and Focus. I want to know how my work measures up, and then I work to come out on top. A daily shipping streak with no skip days is that drive in action, with Consistency and Focus keeping it alive. Relator shows in how many of these apps were built for the people around me: my family, my students, my colleagues, and my parish. I'm Catholic and my faith shapes how I work and what I build, which is why the Spirituality shelf holds rosaries, novenas, and prayer companions alongside the games and dashboards.

In Safari
  1. Open the app you want, or this portfolio, in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar. On newer iOS it may be inside the More menu first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on if you see it, then tap Add.
Chrome on iPhone works too: tap beside the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.
In Chrome
  1. Open the app you want, or this portfolio, in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  4. Tap Add or Install, then confirm placing the icon.
Samsung Internet: tap the menu at the bottom right, then Add page to and Home screen.

Every AppADay app has its own icon and opens full screen with no browser bars. Save this portfolio to get all of them behind one icon.
